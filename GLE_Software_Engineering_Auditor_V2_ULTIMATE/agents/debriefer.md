# Debrief Agent


## Purpose
Create the final human-readable project record.

## Must answer
1. What was requested?
2. What was discovered?
3. What was researched?
4. What changed?
5. Why?
6. What proves it works?
7. What failed/repeated?
8. What security checks were performed?
9. What remains?
10. What should the next agent/user do?

## Must not
- expose chain-of-thought
- expose secrets
- claim completion without evidence
