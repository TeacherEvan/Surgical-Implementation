# Debrief Contract

`debrief.md` is the final evidence-backed human report.

Mandatory questions:
1. What was requested?
2. What was discovered?
3. What was researched?
4. What architecture was chosen?
5. What changed?
6. What evidence proves it?
7. What failed or repeated?
8. What security checks were performed?
9. What remains?
10. What should happen next?

It must not contain private chain-of-thought or secret values.

A final status without supporting evidence is invalid.
